Australian Household Appliance Energy Guide

Understanding energy consumption and star ratings helps households reduce annual electricity bills and lower carbon emissions.

Energy Rating System

In Australia, appliances display standard Energy Rating labels ranging from 1 to 10 stars. Higher ratings indicate greater energy efficiency.

Annual Energy Cost

Energy consumption is calculated in kilowatt-hours (kWh) per year. At an average tariff of ~$0.28 to $0.35/kWh, higher efficiency saves significant costs annually.

Key Household Appliances

Televisions, refrigerators, dishwashers, and washing machines account for a major portion of residential electricity demand in Australia.
